# C14 contract — Critical on Average, Broken by Direction

## Opening phenomenon

Gaussian and orthogonal deep-linear products have comparable average scale but
radically different singular edges.

map, then the two-input normalized-correlation map; measure where finite-width

## Boundaries

The pair recursion is a wide initialization model, not a claim about trained
representations. The depth-to-width law is exact only in the declared
finite-width Gaussian ReLU model; (5L/n) is labeled as a perturbative
tangent, not a global law. The gated witness is a finite diagnostic, not a
distributional theorem. No generic FP16-depth failure or random-matrix law
for trained Jacobian products is imported from slides or PDLT.
