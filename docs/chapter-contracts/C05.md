# C05 Contract — The Safe Zone Is Logarithmic

## Identity

**Unit ID:** C05
**Act:** Act I — Geometry of high dimensions
**Working title:** The Safe Zone Is Logarithmic
**Student transformation:** After this chapter, the reader can turn a
one-variable tail envelope into simultaneous control of many coordinates and
weighted sums because they understand the MGF method, sub-Gaussian proxy
scale, Orlicz diagnostic, and union-bound budget.

**Thesis:** High dimension is manageable when deviations pay an exponential
price: the threshold for controlling \(m\) sub-Gaussian quantities grows like
\(\sqrt{\log m}\), not \(\sqrt{m}\).

## Opening phenomenon

Generate independent standard-normal vectors of lengths
\(10^2,\ldots,10^6\). In the seeded laptop witness, the largest absolute
coordinate grows from about 2.50 to 4.71. The exact 99%-simultaneous
sub-Gaussian threshold at \(10^6\) is 6.18. The same model calculation at one
billion coordinates is 7.21; no billion-coordinate sample is claimed.

**Prediction:** Does multiplying the number of coordinates by ten multiply
the simultaneous deviation threshold by ten, by \(\sqrt{10}\), or only add a
small amount?

**Phenomenon ID:** `c05-simultaneous-safe-zone`
**Hardware class:** (a), laptop-CPU reproducible.

## Causal chain

1. **Problem:** Coordinatewise randomness must remain predictable across a
   large collection.
2. **Instrumentation:** Plot observed maxima beside Chebyshev and
   sub-Gaussian simultaneous thresholds.
3. **Diagnosis:** A finite variance gives polynomial tail cost; simultaneous
   control needs an exponential tail envelope at the scale in question.
4. **Theory:** Apply Markov's inequality after exponentiation, optimize the
   free parameter, close the class under independent sums, and union-bound
   across coordinates.
5. **Solution:** Normalize coefficient energy so weighted sums retain an
   order-one proxy scale as width grows.
6. **Audit:** Distinguish a proxy variance from actual variance, a high-
   probability statement from a deterministic bound, and a finite sample tail
   diagnostic from proof of sub-Gaussianity.

## Theory and theorem ledger

### `thm-c05-mgf-tail`

- **Phenomenon explained:** `c05-simultaneous-safe-zone`
- **Assumptions:** centered \(X\) and
  \(\E e^{\lambda X}\le e^{\sigma^2\lambda^2/2}\) for all real \(\lambda\).
- **Conclusion:** \(\Pr(|X|\ge t)\le
  2e^{-t^2/(2\sigma^2)}\).
- **Proof mode:** full proof.
- **Diagnostic corollary:** the MGF envelope converts deviation control into a
  one-dimensional optimization.
- **Boundary:** \(\sigma^2\) is a proxy and need not be the minimum or equal
  the variance.
- **Source:** Chernoff and Vershynin.

### `thm-c05-independent-sum`

- **Phenomenon explained:** `c05-simultaneous-safe-zone`
- **Assumptions:** independent centered variables with declared MGF proxy
  variances \(\sigma_i^2\).
- **Conclusion:** their sum has proxy variance \(\sum_i\sigma_i^2\); a weighted
  sum has proxy variance \(\sum_i a_i^2\sigma_i^2\).
- **Proof mode:** full proof.
- **Diagnostic corollary:** coefficient \(\ell_2\)-energy, not term count by
  itself, controls a weighted sum.
- **Boundary:** dependence prevents MGF factorization without further
  structure.
- **Source:** Hoeffding and Vershynin.

### `thm-c05-simultaneous-control`

- **Phenomenon explained:** `c05-simultaneous-safe-zone`
- **Assumptions:** \(m\) variables each satisfy the same two-sided
  sub-Gaussian tail bound; independence across the \(m\) variables is not
  required.
- **Conclusion:** with probability at least \(1-\delta\),
  \[
  \max_j |X_j|
  \le \sigma\sqrt{2\log(2m/\delta)}.
  \]
- **Proof mode:** full proof by union bound.
- **Diagnostic corollary:** simultaneous thresholds pay \(\sqrt{\log m}\).
- **Boundary:** dependence changes joint behavior but not this union-bound
  guarantee; the result may be loose.
- **Source:** direct corollary of the MGF tail theorem.

### `thm-c05-equivalent-diagnostics`

- **Phenomenon explained:** `c05-simultaneous-safe-zone`
- **Assumptions:** centered real random variable.
- **Conclusion:** Gaussian tail decay, \(\sqrt p\) moment growth, finite
  exponential-square moment, and quadratic MGF growth are equivalent up to
  universal constants.
- **Proof mode:** proof-with-pointer; one direction is checked explicitly.
- **Diagnostic corollary:** the reader may choose the characterization that
  matches the available evidence or algebra.
- **Boundary:** finite empirical moments do not certify the population tail
  class.
- **Source:** Vershynin, Proposition 2.5.2.
