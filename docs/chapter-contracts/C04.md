# C04 Contract — One Step, Many Clocks

## Identity

**Unit ID:** C04
**Act:** Act 0 — The computational arena
**Working title:** One Step, Many Clocks
**Student transformation:** After this chapter, the reader can diagnose a
gradient trajectory mode by mode because they can read stability, oscillation,
conditioning, preconditioning, and a stochastic floor directly from the
spectrum of a quadratic control problem.

**Thesis:** A scalar learning rate creates a different clock in every
eigendirection; the sharpest direction limits the step, the flattest direction
sets the wait, and stochastic forcing replaces a point endpoint with a
distribution.

## Opening phenomenon

For

\[
\loss(\vect{e})=\tfrac12\vect{e}^{\mathsf T}
\operatorname{diag}(1,100)\vect{e},
\qquad
\vect{e}_0=(1,1)^{\mathsf T},
\]

use the stable step \(\alpha=0.019\). The two coordinates receive multipliers
\(0.981\) and \(-0.9\). The sharp coordinate alternates sign while decaying;
the flat coordinate never changes sign and requires 361 steps to fall below
\(10^{-3}\). A single decreasing objective hides both behaviors.

**Prediction:** Which coordinate limits the safe step, which coordinate
determines the stopping time, and which observation would falsify the fixed
quadratic diagnosis?

**Phenomenon ID:** `c04-many-clocks`
**Hardware class:** (a), laptop-CPU reproducible.

## Causal chain

1. **Problem:** One stable update produces oscillation and apparent stalling at
   the same time.
2. **Instrumentation:** Rotate the error into Hessian eigenvectors and record
   each scalar multiplier.
3. **Diagnosis:** The update matrix is \(\matr{I}-\alpha\matr{Q}\); its spectral
   radius, not the gradient norm alone, controls the linear recurrence.
4. **Theory:** Derive exact eigendirection dynamics, the interval
   \(0<\alpha<2/\lambda_{\max}\), the optimal constant-step contraction, and
   the exact scalar second moment under additive gradient noise.
5. **Solution:** Compress the effective spectrum with a declared positive
   preconditioner and anneal only when the stochastic floor, not a transient
   mode, limits progress.
6. **Audit:** Separate iteration count from wall-clock time; separate a fixed
   Hessian from changing curvature; compare a stochastic floor with C03's
   representational floor.

## Theory and theorem ledger

### `thm-c04-mode-dynamics`

- **Phenomenon explained:** `c04-many-clocks`
- **Assumptions:** \(\matr{Q}\) is real symmetric positive definite; exact
  arithmetic; constant step; centered quadratic.
- **Conclusion:** In an orthonormal eigenbasis,
  \(z_{k,t}=(1-\alpha\lambda_k)^t z_{k,0}\), with convergence exactly when
  \(0<\alpha<2/\lambda_{\max}\).
- **Proof mode:** full proof.
- **Diagnostic corollary:** sign and magnitude of \(1-\alpha\lambda_k\)
  distinguish monotone decay, oscillation, boundary behavior, and divergence.
- **Boundary:** a changing or indefinite Hessian is not this recurrence.
- **Source:** Nesterov plus independent derivation.

### `thm-c04-optimal-step`

- **Phenomenon explained:** `c04-many-clocks`
- **Assumptions:** spectrum contained in
  \([\lambda_{\min},\lambda_{\max}]\), constant scalar step, Euclidean error.
- **Conclusion:** The minimax constant step is
  \(2/(\lambda_{\max}+\lambda_{\min})\), with contraction
  \((\kappa-1)/(\kappa+1)\).
- **Proof mode:** full proof.
- **Diagnostic corollary:** even the best scalar step pays a rate determined by
  the spectral spread.
- **Boundary:** accelerated, nonstationary, and preconditioned methods use a
  different polynomial or operator. The current-direction line minimizer
  answers a different one-step question and may exceed the fixed global
  stability threshold.
- **Source:** Nesterov plus independent derivation.

The manuscript also derives the exact current-direction line minimizer
\(\|g_t\|_2^2/(g_t^{\mathsf T}Qg_t)\). This is a derivation, not a fourth
theorem entry. Its job is to prevent “optimal step size” from silently
collapsing fixed minimax, current-direction, and finite-horizon objectives.

### `thm-c04-noisy-second-moment`

- **Phenomenon explained:** `c04-many-clocks`
- **Assumptions:** scalar positive curvature, stable constant step,
  independent zero-mean additive gradient noise with variance \(\tau^2\).
- **Conclusion:** The exact second moment is a decaying transient plus
  \[
  \frac{\alpha\tau^2}{\lambda(2-\alpha\lambda)}
  \left[1-(1-\alpha\lambda)^{2t}\right].
  \]
- **Proof mode:** full proof.
- **Diagnostic corollary:** constant-step noise creates a calculable
  algorithmic floor distinct from finite-grid stagnation.
- **Boundary:** state-dependent, correlated, multiplicative, or heavy-tailed
  noise requires a different model.
- **Source:** stochastic-approximation context from Robbins–Monro; exact
  recurrence derived here.
