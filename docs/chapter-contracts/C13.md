# C13 contract — The Mean Gradient Is Not the Regime

## Opening phenomenon

Two conditionally unbiased scalar estimators share a mean gradient and step
size but produce expected descent and expected ascent because their conditional
variances differ.

## Diagnostic chain

Declare $\mathcal F_k$, estimator target, and conditional second moment;
derive the smoothness bound; expose $\chi_k$; separate batch scaling from its
independence assumption; audit clipping bias.
